# Module Settings

Open **Configure Settings** and pick the *XCrawl Classics System* section. World-scoped settings apply to everyone and can only be changed by the GM; user-scoped settings are per-player.

## Automation
* **Automate Messenger Disapproval** — automatically apply Disapproval on failed Holy Act checks. *(on by default)*
* **Automate Mojo** — automatically track Mojo based on roll results: a natural 20 awards the rolling player a point, a natural 1 clears everyone's Mojo. See [Mojo](Mojo.md). *(on by default)*
* **Reuse Half-Orc's Deed Die** — use the Deed Die result from the last attack for the Half-Orc's Wild Attack instead of re-rolling it. *(on by default)*

## Equipment Tab Conveniences
These add class abilities as weapon rows on the equipment tab for ease of access:

* **Athlete's Grapple as a Weapon** *(off by default)*
* **Brawler's Unarmed as a Weapon** *(off by default)*
* **Dwarf's Shield Bash as a Weapon** *(on by default)*

## Interface
* **Hide Notes Tab** — hides the notes tab on the actor sheet to compensate for the additional Xcrawl tab and keep the sheet width manageable; a smaller replacement notes field lives in the Xcrawl tab. *(per-user, on by default)*
* **Smaller Pause** — a smaller, less intrusive version of the XCrawl TV pause animation. *(per-user, off by default)*

## Development
* **Debug Mode** — prints debug information about objects and events to the browser console. Intended for module developers. *(off by default)*
